## What was deferred (follow-ups)

- **TZ-240.A** — Service-layer `.findAll()` $or-filter on Product/Material/Category services. Must thread `userOrgId` through controller → service signature; risk of call-site breakage. Architecturally required per AC #2.
- **TZ-240.B** — Composite unique index `(organizationId, sku)` for Product / Material. Per thinker audit: HIGH risk in autonomous mode — Mongoose auto-index would crash at startup unless the existing `sku_1` global unique index is manually dropped first.
- **TZ-240.C** — `isSystem` mass-assignment guard. Per code-reviewer round 3: any client that POSTs/PATCHes products can currently set `isSystem: true` and bypass org-scoping. Mitigation: enforce `@Roles('admin')` on isSystem-modifying paths, or derive from `req.user` context, ignoring client field.
